Welcome to FileEngine. This help center explains the parts of the app that are
powerful but not always obvious. You can open it any time from the **?** button in
the top bar, and the **ⓘ** icons scattered next to features jump you straight to the
relevant topic.

## The basics

- **Files** — browse, upload, and organize your documents and folders. Every item
  has an owner and a set of permissions that decide who can see and change it.
- **Search** — find files by name and by their indexed contents *and metadata*,
  including engineering and CAD/BIM models.
- **Chat** — an AI research assistant that can answer questions using your
  documents and the web.
